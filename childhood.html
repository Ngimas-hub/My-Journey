Formative Years

Growing up in the city, like every other child I spent most days helping with chores at home, exploring the estate and making new friends.
Most days while watching I always wondered how these shows were programmed, how the people got into the TV , how different screens displayed differently and how remotes worked and that sparked something in me. The urge to know and interact with this technology.

Memorable moments

My most memorable moment was when I would visit my grandparents in the village, they would tell us stories around the fire and make us learn traditional folksongs and dance.
Another core memory is when we'd watch TV in groups and later discuss the programs. We would organize quizzes and debates which were always fun and educative.
